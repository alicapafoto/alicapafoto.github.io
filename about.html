Ali Capa

Photographer  ·  Porto, Portugal

Ali Capa Foto
est. 2015

I am a fine art photographer whose work explores travel, memory, intimacy and the human experience. Through portraiture, landscapes and documentary influenced photography, I search for the quiet moments that shape how we remember places, people and ourselves.

I was born in 1989 in Maracaibo, Venezuela. I grew up between Salt Lake City and Calgary, where an early fascination with art, drawing and music led me into the underground music scene in Utah. After completing my studies in the United States, I graduated from Universidad Católica Cecilio Acosta with a Bachelor’s degree in Communications and an Associate of Arts degree.

I have worked across film productions and photographic studios, developing a visual language defined by saturated colour, painterly movement, dreams and intimacy. Alongside my commercial and cinematic work, I have continued creating personal projects through staged photographic narratives, portraiture, landscapes and fine art nude photography.

I currently live in Portugal.

I have always believed that photographs can do more than document a moment.

They can remind us of someone we miss, encourage us to travel somewhere unknown, or reveal emotions we didn't know we were carrying.

My work moves between landscapes and the human body because I see both as expressions of the same thing. Desire. Memory. Freedom. Longing. Home.

Whether I am photographing a mountain road in Portugal or a quiet portrait in Venezuela, I am searching for photographs that continue living long after the shutter closes.

If someone leaves my work wanting to write a poem, call an old friend, take a journey, or simply pay closer attention to the world around them, then the photograph has done its job.

Photography has taken me across ten countries and introduced me to extraordinary people, but I'm still chasing the same thing I was looking for when I first picked up a camera.

A feeling worth remembering.

Come with me.

I'll show you something beautiful.
