# Safety Rules for Any Helper

## Never share or copy

- passwords;
- API keys;
- webhook secrets;
- recovery codes;
- private keys;
- complete card details;
- customer records;
- Ali’s private sender address or telephone;
- private administrator links or identifiers.

## Never take these actions casually

- Do not upload the preproduction Original Works package as the live website.
- Do not change a price, edition, SKU, size, paper, provider, availability state, or delivery rule without Ali’s approval.
- Do not mark a unique Original Artwork available from memory or from a public page.
- Do not process an order merely because an email says payment succeeded.
- Do not place a supplier order before funds are available in Wise unless Ali explicitly approves it.
- Do not create a second supplier order or carrier label because a page was slow.
- Do not restore a contribution, donation, or tip feature without written Stripe approval.
- Do not bypass Stripe, Cloudflare, DHL, the database, or the signed webhook.
- Do not merge a Draft Pull Request or deploy a working branch without Ali’s explicit approval.

## Stop immediately when

- payment records disagree;
- an order appears twice;
- one unique artwork appears connected to two collectors;
- a paid artwork does not show Sold;
- the delivery address or quote changed unexpectedly;
- the provider product or SKU does not match the order;
- a compliance or identity warning appears;
- private data appears in a public file;
- the correct production branch or archive is unclear.

The safe response to uncertainty is to pause the affected action, preserve the records, and ask Ali. A delayed order is safer than duplicate fulfilment, a double sale, an incorrect refund, or a public security incident.
