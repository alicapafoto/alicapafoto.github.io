# Orders, Money and Shipping in Plain Language

## Money flow

Stripe receives the customer’s card payment and later pays the money into Wise. Delivery paid by the customer is tracked separately from the artwork or product price and is not treated as profit.

Ali waits until the payout is visibly available in Wise before placing a manual printer order or moving into the approved shipment step.

## Open-edition prints

- Produced through Prodigi.
- Customer pays delivery.
- Ali manually places the matching order after the payout is available.
- Prodigi ships directly where the approved route supports it.

## Collector-edition prints

- Produced through Creativehub/theprintspace on Hahnemühle Pearl paper.
- Print-only and unframed.
- Certificate carries signature and edition number.
- Ali manually places the order after funds are available.

## Books

- NiNE uses the approved Mixam book records and fulfilment workflow.
- The exact edition and production record must be checked before ordering.

## Original Artworks

- Each exists only once.
- Delivery must be insured.
- Ali packs the framed artwork personally.
- The finished package is measured and weighed before label approval.
- The shipment is created only after Ali confirms the address and parcel.
- Ali takes the parcel to the approved DHL carrier/drop-off point.

## Order records

The operational order view tracks the product, payment, Wise status, supplier, delivery charged, supplier cost, destination, provider order ID, tracking, and final status.

The order sheet helps Ali work, but it is not proof of payment by itself. Stripe and the protected backend record must agree.

## Refunds and problems

- Refund through the original Stripe payment route.
- Do not send money separately to “make it easier.”
- For damage, preserve photographs of the packaging, label, protection, and item.
- For a wrong address, lost parcel, chargeback, customs issue, or unclear payment, stop and check the records before promising an outcome.
