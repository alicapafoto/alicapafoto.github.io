## Authoritative decisions

- Safety takes priority over speed. Original Artworks will not open merely because the interface is ready.
- Delivery is quoted before an artwork is reserved.
- Stripe Checkout creation and the D1 reservation form one protected 30-minute purchase window.
- A checkout-created artwork cannot be released by generic timer cleanup.
- Successful verified payment permanently changes a unique artwork to sold.
- DHL shipment and label creation remains a private manual action after Ali physically packs and measures the artwork.
- All Original Artworks remain €200 with €200 declared and insured value.
- The provisional parcel profile remains 55 × 45 × 15 cm and 2.5 kg until the first real packed parcel is measured.
- Sender address and telephone are private configuration values and must not appear in shareable documentation or GitHub.
- The one-time contribution feature must not return without written Stripe approval.
- Patreon remains an external membership route and is not part of the removed Stripe contribution feature.
- Operational Diary entries are created after meaningful workdays, organized by year and month, with no weekly folders.
- Gmail state labels show the next action; context labels show the business area. Completed threads lose active state labels and move to the Reference Archive.
