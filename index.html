Porto, Portugal — est. 2015

NiNE is a 132 page fine art photobook by photographer Ali Capa, documenting nine years of travel, portraiture, landscapes, and fine art nude photography across ten countries.

Portraiture, landscape, and intimate nude photography collide in a visual dialogue between chaos and tenderness, between what is barely seen and what is deeply felt.

This is not a timeline. It is a closing. A body of work that moves through unrest, desire, memory, and the quiet weight of survival, arriving finally at something singular: Sensation.
