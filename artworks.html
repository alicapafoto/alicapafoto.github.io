The process

From idea to photograph.
From photograph to emulsion.
From emulsion into physical form.

These works began as photographs and were transformed by hand. The photographic emulsion was transferred onto wood, then worked with 24 karat gold and acrylic paint before being sealed with varnish.

Each piece is offered as a unique original. The framed work is the final work, ready to live beyond the screen and enter a physical space.
